How Stackable Discounts Actually Work

Retailers frequently advertise promotions such as "30% OFF plus an extra 10% at checkout". Many shoppers mistakenly assume this equals a 40% total discount ($30\% + 10\%$). In reality, retailers calculate secondary discounts sequentially.

The primary 30% discount is applied first to the original price. Then, the extra 10% coupon is applied to the reduced subtotal, not the original price. This produces an effective discount of 37% ($1 - 0.70 \times 0.90 = 0.37$), saving you ₹924.63 instead of ₹999.60 on a ₹2,499 purchase.

Single Discount:
• Savings = Original Price × (Discount % ÷ 100)
• Sale Price = Original Price − Savings

Double / Sequential Discount:
• Intermediate Price = Original Price × [1 − (D₁ ÷ 100)]
• Final Price = Intermediate Price × [1 − (D₂ ÷ 100)]
• Effective Discount % = [1 − (1 − D₁/100) × (1 − D₂/100)] × 100

Worked Example: ₹2,499 Item with 30% + 10% Promo

Step-by-Step Breakdown

  1. Original Price = ₹2,499.00
  2. Primary 30% discount = $2499 \times 0.30 =$ ₹749.70
  3. Price after primary discount = ₹2,499 - ₹749.70 = ₹1,749.30
  4. Secondary 10% coupon = $1749.30 \times 0.10 =$ ₹174.93
  5. Final Price = ₹1,749.30 - ₹174.93 = ₹1,574.37
  6. Total Savings = ₹749.70 + ₹174.93 = ₹924.63 (37.0%)
