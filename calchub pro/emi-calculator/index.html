How EMI is Calculated

An Equated Monthly Instalment (EMI) is the fixed monthly amount paid by a borrower to a lender to repay a debt. Each EMI payment is structured to cover both the interest accrued on the outstanding balance and a portion of the principal loan amount.

In standard retail lending (including home loans, auto loans, and personal loans), banks utilize the reducing balance method. In the initial tenure, the interest component accounts for a substantial proportion of the EMI. As payments are made, the principal balance shrinks, reducing the interest charges and accelerating principal reduction.

EMI = [P × r × (1 + r)ⁿ] ÷ [(1 + r)ⁿ − 1] Where:
• P = Principal loan amount borrowed
• r = Periodic monthly interest rate (Annual Interest Rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100)
• n = Total number of monthly installments (Tenure in years × 12)

Worked Example: EMI on a ₹10 Lakh Loan

Loan Scenario

Consider taking a personal or auto loan with the following parameters:

  • Principal Amount (P): ₹10,00,000
  • Annual Interest Rate (R): 9.5% per annum
  • Loan Tenure: 5 years (60 months)

Calculation Breakdown

  1. Monthly interest rate: $r = \frac{9.5}{12 \times 100} = 0.0079167$
  2. Number of monthly installments: $n = 5 \times 12 = 60$
  3. Factor: $(1 + r)^{60} = (1.0079167)^{60} \approx 1.605009$
  4. Numerator: $10,00,000 \times 0.0079167 \times 1.605009 \approx 12,706.33$
  5. Denominator: $1.605009 - 1 = 0.605009$
  6. Monthly EMI: $12,706.33 \div 0.605009 \approx$ ₹21,002
  7. Total Payment over 5 years: $21,002 \times 60 =$ ₹12,60,117
  8. Total Interest Paid: $12,60,117 - 10,00,000 =$ ₹2,60,117

Important Assumptions & Limitations

Reducing Balance Assumption: Calculations assume fixed periodic compounding on a reducing balance basis. If your bank applies a flat interest rate or changes floating rates during the tenure, actual monthly figures will vary.

Our EMI calculator computes pure principal and interest obligations. Keep the following factors in mind when taking an actual loan:

  • Processing Fees: Most lenders charge 0.25% to 2% upfront processing fees plus GST.
  • Prepayment Rules: RBI mandates zero foreclosure charges on floating-rate individual home loans, but fixed-rate or commercial loans may carry prepayment penalties.
  • Floating vs Fixed: Floating interest rates fluctuate with the RBI repo rate or lender MCLR/EBLR benchmarks.

Tips to Reduce Your Total Loan Cost

Tip 1: Pay one extra EMI each year. Making just one additional monthly payment per calendar year can trim 3 to 4 years off a 20-year home loan and save lakhs in compound interest.

Tip 2: Prioritize shorter tenures when budget permits. While extending your tenure from 15 to 25 years reduces your EMI slightly, it nearly doubles the total interest paid to the bank.
