All Three Percentage Formulas Explained

A percentage represents a portion out of 100 (from the Latin per centum). It provides a universal metric to compare fractions, growth rates, test scores, and price fluctuations across differing scales.

Formula 1 (Percentage of a Number):
• Result = (X × Y) ÷ 100

Formula 2 (Portion as a Percentage):
• Percentage = (X ÷ Y) × 100

Formula 3 (Percentage Increase / Decrease):
• Percentage Change = [(Final − Initial) ÷ Initial] × 100 If Percentage Change is positive, it represents an increase.
If Percentage Change is negative, it represents a decrease / reduction.

Frequently Asked Questions

To find X% of Y, multiply the number Y by X and divide the result by 100. Formula: Result = (X × Y) / 100. For example, 15% of 200 = (15 × 200) / 100 = 30.

To find what percentage X is of Y, divide X by Y and multiply by 100. Formula: Percentage = (X / Y) × 100. For example, 45 out of 180 is (45 / 180) × 100 = 25%.

Formula: Percentage Change = ((New Value - Old Value) / Old Value) × 100. A positive result indicates a percentage increase, while a negative result represents a percentage decrease.
