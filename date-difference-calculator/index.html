Inclusive vs. Exclusive Date Calculations

When calculating the difference between dates, context determines whether the final day should be included:

  • Standard Exclusive Interval: If you check into a hotel on June 1 and check out on June 5, you stayed for 4 nights (June 5 - June 1 = 4).
  • Inclusive Count: If you are serving a jury duty or employment contract from June 1 through June 5, you worked 5 full days (June 1, 2, 3, 4, and 5). Checking the "Include end date" box accommodates this scenario.

How the Breakdown Is Counted

  • Months and days: whole calendar months are counted forward from the start date; if the start day does not exist in a shorter month (31 January into February), the last day of that month is used. For example, 31 January to 1 March is 1 month and 1 day, and also 29 days in total.
  • Weekdays: the Monday-to-Friday count does not remove public holidays or local working-week differences.
  • Visa and legal limits: official rules, such as rolling 90-in-180-day stay limits, are more complex than a simple count and are not calculated here. Check the rules of the authority concerned.
