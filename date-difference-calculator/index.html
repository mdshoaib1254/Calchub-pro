Inclusive vs. Exclusive Date Calculations

When calculating the difference between dates, context determines whether the final day should be included:

  • Standard Exclusive Interval: If you check into a hotel on June 1 and check out on June 5, you stayed for 4 nights (June 5 - June 1 = 4).
  • Inclusive Count: If you are serving a jury duty or employment contract from June 1 through June 5, you worked 5 full days (June 1, 2, 3, 4, and 5). Checking the "Include end date" box accommodates this scenario.
