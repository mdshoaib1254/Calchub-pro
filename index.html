Our loan tools (Home Loan, Car Loan, Personal Loan, and General EMI) use the reducing-balance amortization formula: EMI = [P × r × (1+r)^n] / [(1+r)^n - 1]. Here, P represents the principal amount borrowed, r is the periodic monthly interest rate (annual rate divided by 12 and 100), and n is the total number of monthly payments. This matches standard banking formulas used by HDFC, SBI, ICICI, and Axis Bank.

When you select "Add GST (Exclusive)", the tax amount is calculated as (Base Price × Rate) / 100 and added on top. When you select "Remove GST (Inclusive)", the base cost is extracted using Base = (Gross Price × 100) / (100 + Rate), and the remaining difference represents the GST amount. Both methods automatically split the tax into equal CGST and SGST portions for intrastate transactions.
